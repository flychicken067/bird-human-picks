Udbredelse

arid habitats near water in the Sahel from Mauritania and Guinea-Bissau through southern Algeria and northern Cameroon to Sudan, Ethiopia, and Somalia, and southwestern Arabian Peninsula, with recent northward expansion along Red Sea coasts of Egypt and Saudi Arabia; widely introduced to New Zealand, Puerto Rico, New Providence (Bahamas), Mexico (Guerrero), Spain, and the Canary Islands as "Ringed Turtle Dove," escapees often noted in the southern USA and southern Europe (engelsk originaltekst)
