Udbredelse

breeds on cliffs of high Arctic to temperate north coasts, from Greenland, Iceland, western Europe, Svalbard (north of Norway), north Siberian island to Sakhalin, Kamchatka, Kuril and Commander islands (eastern Russia); Aleutian and Bering Sea islands, western and southwestern Alaska mainland; Canadian high Arctic (northeastern Canada), Gulf of St. Lawrence and Newfoundland (southeastern Canada); winters to western North Atlantic coast and coastal waters south to Virginia; mid-Atlantic Ridge to Azores; North Sea; Japan (southward to Honshu) and Korean Peninsula, and southeastern Alaska to southern California (engelsk originaltekst)
