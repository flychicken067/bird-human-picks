Udbredelse

humid to deciduous forest and open habitats from central-west Panama eastward to the lowlands of northern South America in northern Colombia and Venezuela to Trinidad and Tobago; in central Colombia southward in the Cauca and Magdalena valleys (to Cauca and Huila), and west of the Andes in western Ecuador and far northwestern Peru; east of the Andes across Venezuela and the Guianas southwestward to western Amazonia in eastern Colombia, Ecuador, Peru, and northern Bolivia, and eastward across the Amazon basin of Brazil southward to Mato Grosso and Mato Grosso do Sul and northeastern Bolivia; also in interior Brazil and in the Atlantic Forest of eastern Brazil southward to Paraguay, northeastern Argentina, and eastern Uruguay (engelsk originaltekst)
