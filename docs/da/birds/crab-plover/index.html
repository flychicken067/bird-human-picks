Udbredelse

coasts of Indian Ocean, breeding on shores of northwestern Indian Ocean, Persian Gulf, and Red Sea; non-breeders regularly range southward to Kenya, Tanzania, and Madagascar, and eastward to western India, Sri Lanka, Maldives, Chagos, and Andaman Islands (possibly breeds in the last four sites); also small numbers to northeastern South Africa, and to southwestern Thailand (engelsk originaltekst)
