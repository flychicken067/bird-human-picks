Udbredelse

lowland to montane grassland, farmland, heath, steppe, and meadows, from British Isles and Scandinavia eastward to eastern Siberia and Kamchatka, and southward to northwestern Africa, southern Europe, northern Iran, and Japan; has bred Pribilof Islands, Alaska. Northern populations migrate southward to northern Africa, Middle East, northern India, and eastern China; introduced in Australia, New Zealand, Hawaiian Islands (all main islands), and Vancouver Island, British Columbia (engelsk originaltekst)
