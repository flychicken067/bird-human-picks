disjunctly in lowland to submontane woodland, savanna, steppe, and plantations, in sub-Saharan Africa from Senegambia eastward to Somalia, and southwestern Arabian Peninsula, and southward (avoiding rainforest) to Namibia and northern South Africa, and in Asia from Armenia, northern Iran, and Kazakhstan eastward through sub-Himalayan India and Sri Lanka, to southern China (including Hainan), and southward to Thailand and Vietnam; some populations migrate to Middle East and Sumatra in winter (engelsk originaltekst)
