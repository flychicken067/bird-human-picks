Distribuzione

southern Europe eastward to Iran and Africa, Madagascar, Seychelles (including Aldabra), Comoros, and Mauritius; colonized the New World in the 20th century, now widespread in North and South America; introduced to the Hawaiian Islands (Northwestern and all main islands); partially migratory in North America and southern Africa (testo originale in inglese)
