breeds low (locally high) Arctic to temperate wet tundra of Holarctic, from southwestern and northeastern Greenland, Iceland, Faroe Islands, northern British Isles, Scandinavia, Baltic region east through northern Russia including Novaya Zemlya (north of northwestern Siberia) and New Siberian Islands (north of east-central Russia), through Chukotskiy Peninsula, western Kamchatka, northern Kurile and northern Sakhalin islands (eastern Russia); Aleutians, western and northern Alaska and northwestern Canada, and north-central Canada north and west of Hudson Bay; winters coastal and inland wetlands from temperate to tropical zones mainly north of Equator, from British Isles, western Europe, Mediterranean, northern and north-central Africa, Middle East, and northern plains of India to northern Vietnam and eastern China, Taiwan, Korean Peninsula, and Japan (Honshu to Ryukyu Islands); southwestern Canada and New England to west-central Mexico and Yucatan Peninsula (영어 원문)
