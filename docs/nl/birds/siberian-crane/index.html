Verspreiding

breeds Arctic east-central Siberia (Yakutia) and western Siberia (at least formerly basin of Kunovat River; also basins of Konda and Alymka rivers); winters very locally on southern shore of Caspian Sea in north-central Iran, with most wintering on middle Yangtze River (mainly Poyang Lake) in east-central China; formerly also Keoladeo (Bharatpur) in northwestern India, but not recorded since 2002 (oorspronkelijke Engelse tekst)
