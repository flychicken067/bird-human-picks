Boobies and Gannets · Sulidae · Suliformes

4 чел. · 3 мес.

Распространение

widespread tropical and subtropical ocean islands; Cabo Verde, Boatswain Bird Island (just off Ascension, south-central South Atlantic Ocean), Alcatraz Island (west of northwestern Guinea), and Gulf of Guinea islands, Caribbean islands off Yucatan Peninsula and Honduras to Panama and northern Colombia, Bahamas and Cayman Islands to Lesser Antilles, islands north of Venezuela and Tobago and off Brazil, islands off Somalia and Socotra, Aldabra, and Madagascar, and Chagos Archipelago, Cocos (Keeling), and Christmas islands (tropical Indian Ocean), islands off northern Australia, Tench (St. Matthias group, north-central Bismarck Archipelago), remote islands in Melanesia, southern Japan (Ryukyu, Izu, Ogasawara, and Iwo islands), Taiwan, offshore islands of Philippines, Vietnam, and Malaysia, in Flores and Banda seas, from Marianas and Palau through Hawaiian Islands (Northwestern and islets off main islands) and Gambier Islands (eastern Tuamotu Archipelago; eastern Polynesia) (оригинальный текст на английском)
