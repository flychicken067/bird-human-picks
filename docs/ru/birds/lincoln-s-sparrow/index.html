Распространение

breeds in boggy woodland and willows from western Alaska to Newfoundland (northeastern Canada), southward through mountains of western USA to southern California and northern New Mexico, and southward in eastern Canada to northern Michigan, Wisconsin and Minnesota; winters in shrubby woodland along the Pacific coast of the USA southward to Baja California and from the southern USA across most of Mexico (rare on Yucatan Peninsula), southeastward to southeastern Honduras (оригинальный текст на английском)
