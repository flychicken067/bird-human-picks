Распространение

breeds high Arctic tundra wetlands of Holarctic from northwestern and southeastern Greenland, Svalbard (north of Norway), north Taymyr Peninsula and Severnaya Zemlya (to the north), Lena Delta, and New Siberian Islands (north-central Siberia), Alaskan North Slope (rare), Canadian Arctic islands from Banks to Ellesmere and south to Southampton; winters sandy coastal beaches worldwide from temperate to tropical zone, in Old World from the British Isles and Atlantic islands through Africa, Malagasy region, Middle East, southern Asia through Indonesian Archipelago to Australia, Melanesia, Philippines, and southern and central Japan (but rare New Guinea and New Zealand); in New World, southwestern and southeastern Canada and Caribbean southward to Tierra del Fuego, and including remote islands of eastern Polynesia (оригинальный текст на английском)
