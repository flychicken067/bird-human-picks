Распространение

nearly cosmopolitan, breeding in open grassy habitats including marshes, tundra, savanna, abandoned agricultural land, open montane woodland, and moorland from Iceland (scarce) through northern and central Europe, eastward through northeastern Russia, and southward through Central Asia and northern Mongolia, in Hawaiian Islands (all main islands), Alaska and northern Canada southward through central USA states, the Greater Antilles (except Jamaica), and much of South America (except Amazonia) from Colombia to Tierra del Fuego, including the Galapagos and Falkland Islands; northern populations winter southward to northern and far western Africa, southern Indian Subcontinent, and southern Mexico (оригинальный текст на английском)
